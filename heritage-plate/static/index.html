How we calculate

  • Numbers come from food tables, not AI. Every value is computed from published tables: IFCT 2017 (Indian Food Composition Tables) and USDA SR28. Tap "Where the numbers come from" on any dish to see the exact entries.
  • AI does the cooking ideas. NVIDIA Nemotron reads your recipe into ingredients and grams, suggests swaps that keep the dish recognisable, and plans the week.
  • Good estimates, not lab tests. We count raw weights. Home measures like "1 katori" or "a little ghee" become estimated grams, so treat numbers as close guides.
  • Honest about gaps. If an ingredient isn't in our tables we say so and don't count it. If a swap adds something we can't count, we don't claim a "% less".

Not medical advice. This is general healthy-eating guidance. Please check changes with your parent's doctor or dietitian, especially if they take insulin or blood-pressure medicine.
