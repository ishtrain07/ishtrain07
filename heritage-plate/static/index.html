How we work out the numbers
  • From food tables, not AI. Every number is computed from published tables (IFCT 2017 and USDA SR28). Each dish shows its sources.
  • AI suggests the cooking. NVIDIA Nemotron reads your recipe, suggests swaps that keep the dish familiar, and plans the week.
  • Close estimates. Home measures like "1 katori" become estimated grams, so treat numbers as a guide.
  • Honest about gaps. If an ingredient isn't in our tables we say so and don't count it, and we don't claim a "% less".
