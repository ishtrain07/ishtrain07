Why this helps

When a parent is told to change their diet, families are usually handed a generic Western diet sheet. Home food starts to feel off-limits, the plan doesn't stick, and someone ends up cooking two meals.

  • Swap, don't banKeeps your family's own dishes and makes them lighter.
  • See the real changeBefore and after for the one number that matters: sugar-raising carbs, salt or saturated fat.
  • Plan the weekMeals, a shopping list and a note for them in their own language.
Where the numbers come from, and who it's for

Numbers from food tables, not AI. They come from published tables (India's IFCT 2017 and USDA), and every dish shows its sources. NVIDIA Nemotron models do the cooking ideas: reading recipes, suggesting swaps and planning the week.

For families cooking South Asian, East or Southeast Asian, Continental, Middle Eastern or Latin American food for a parent with type 2 diabetes, high blood pressure or high cholesterol.

How we work out the numbers
  • From food tables, not AI. Every number is computed from published tables (IFCT 2017 and USDA SR28). Each dish shows its sources.
  • AI suggests the cooking. NVIDIA Nemotron reads your recipe, suggests swaps that keep the dish familiar, and plans the week.
  • Close estimates. Home measures like "1 katori" become estimated grams, so treat numbers as a guide.
  • Honest about gaps. If an ingredient isn't in our tables we say so and don't count it, and we don't claim a "% less".
