1 · Mentioned

55 / 60
Small businessBest Apps & Agents track

"Your customers stopped Googling. They ask AI. Does it mention you?"

How it works

Enter a business name, city and type. It asks 25 real customer-style questions ("best bakery near me"), searches the web like an AI assistant, and shows: "AI mentions you in 3 of 25 answers. It recommends these 4 instead, and here's why." Then it writes paste-ready fixes and re-checks weekly.

Why it wins
  • Simplest build: text in, text out
  • Timely: AI is replacing search
  • Natural subscription business
Watch out

Shows visibility, not sales (yet). SEO incumbents may copy it.

2 · Curb Cut

48 / 60
Accessibility / webCoding & Agentic track

"Point it at any website. It fixes what blind and disabled visitors can't use, and proves it."

How it works

It runs an accessibility audit, then the AI rewrites the actual website code: describes images for screen readers, labels buttons, fixes contrast. It re-runs the audit to prove each fix, then hands over the changes. Demo: score 54 → 96, plus a screen reader before and after.

Why it wins
  • Measurable, can't-fake result
  • Real social and legal impact
  • Matches the Coding track brief exactly
Watch out

Most crowded track; less visual for the designer.

3 · Misconception Map

47 / 60
EducationBest Apps & Agents track

"Grading tells a teacher who got it wrong. This tells them why."

How it works

A teacher uploads a class set of quiz answers. The AI groups wrong answers by the misunderstanding behind them, e.g. "11 students think 1/3 is bigger than 1/2 because 3 is bigger than 2", and writes a 10-minute reteach plan plus practice questions for each group.

Why it wins
  • Most creative and human story
  • Not another tutor chatbot
  • Weekly use, huge audience
Watch out

Reading handwriting is hard. Start with typed answers and Google Forms exports.

4 · Caregiver Log

43 / 60
Health / familyPersonal AI track

"Talk for 30 seconds a day about Mom. Walk into the doctor's office with the whole picture."

How it works

A family caregiver leaves short voice notes ("slept badly, skipped lunch, a bit confused"). The assistant tracks patterns over weeks, flags changes worth mentioning, and writes a one-page brief for the next doctor visit. Private by design.

Why it wins
  • Deepest real-life pain
  • Very relatable demo
  • Fits the Personal AI track
Watch out

Needs NVIDIA's newer personal-AI tools; health data needs care.
