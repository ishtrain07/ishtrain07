1 · Mentioned

54 / 60
Apps & AgentsSmall business

"Your customers stopped Googling. They ask AI. Does it mention you?"

How it works

Enter a business name, city and type. It asks 25 customer-style questions, searches the web like an AI assistant, and shows: "AI mentions you in 3 of 25 answers. It recommends these 4 instead, and here's why." Then it writes paste-ready fixes and re-checks weekly.

Why
  • Text in, text out: simplest build
  • Timely, with a clear subscription model
Watch out

Shows visibility, not sales. SEO tools may copy it.

2 · Misconception Map

51 / 60
Apps & AgentsEducation

"Grading tells a teacher who got it wrong. This tells them why."

How it works

A teacher uploads a class's quiz answers (Google Forms export or photos). The AI groups wrong answers by the misunderstanding behind them, e.g. "11 students think 1/3 > 1/2 because 3 > 2", and writes a 10-minute reteach plan plus practice questions per group.

Why
  • Most creative, human story
  • Weekly use; not a tutor chatbot
Watch out

Handwriting is hard. Start with typed answers.

3 · Caregiver Log

48 / 60
Personal AIHealth / family

"Talk for 30 seconds a day about Mom. Walk into the doctor's office with the whole picture."

How it works

A family caregiver leaves short voice notes ("slept badly, skipped lunch, a bit confused"). A private, always-on assistant remembers everything, spots changes over weeks, and writes a one-page brief for the next doctor visit.

Why
  • Deepest real-life pain
  • Exactly the Personal AI brief: memory + privacy
Watch out

New NVIDIA agent tools to learn; health data needs care.

4 · Life Admin

45 / 60
Personal AIEveryday life

"Never miss a return window, renewal or warranty again."

How it works

It reads your receipts and confirmation emails, then keeps one timeline of every deadline: returns closing, subscriptions renewing, passport or licence expiring, warranties you can still claim. It nudges you in time and drafts the cancel / return / renew message for you.

Why
  • Everyone has this problem
  • Uses memory + skills + tools, as the track asks
Watch out

Needs email access; less novel.

5 · Coach's Assistant

45 / 60
Apps & AgentsSports

"For the volunteer coach: talk after the game, get the practice plan and the parent update."

How it works

After a youth or amateur game, the coach records a 2-minute voice note and adds the basic stats. The agent tracks each player's progress over the season and writes next practice's plan, each player's focus area, and the weekly parent email.

Why
  • Light build, fun demo
  • Huge, overlooked audience of volunteer coaches
Watch out

Lower "impact" score with judges than health or education.
