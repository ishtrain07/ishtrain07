How decisions are made

How decisions are made

  1. Market light: S&P 500 vs its 50/200-day averages + VIX. GREEN = up to 3 positions at full risk; YELLOW = half risk, top grades only; RED = no buys.
  2. Filters: price > $10, >$25M traded daily, above the 200-day average. No penny stocks, no downtrends.
  3. Score 0-100 (quant multi-factor, ranked across ~90 names): momentum 30% (6/3/1-month), relative strength vs S&P 15%, trend quality 20%, entry setup 25%, fundamentals 10% (growth, margins, forward P/E vs growth), ±5 for leading/lagging sector.
  4. Setups: PULLBACK = dip to the 20-day EMA in an uptrend that bounces green (RSI 35-58). BREAKOUT = new 20-day high on ≥1.4x volume. Stretched stocks (>3 ATR above EMA or RSI > 76) are never chased.
  5. BUY = score ≥ 65 + setup triggered today + no earnings inside the hold window + slot free (max 2 per sector).
  6. Exits: stop = recent swing low or 1-2 ATR (never more than 8%). Sell half at 1.5x risk and move the stop to breakeven, sell the rest at 3x risk or on the trailing stop. Time stop after 7 days (pullback) / 6 (breakout). Always out before earnings.
  7. Sizing: each trade risks 1.5% of the account (~$30); max 30% in one stock. Account down 10% = stop buying; down 13% = go to cash.

Rules-based signals for personal use, not financial advice. No strategy guarantees profits; every trade can lose money. Size positions so a stop-out never hurts.
