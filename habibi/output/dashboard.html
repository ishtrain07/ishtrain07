How decisions are made
  1. Market light: S&P 500 vs its 50/200-day averages + VIX. RED = sell rotation holdings to cash, no buys.
  2. Universe filters: price > $5, >$25M traded daily, above the 200-day average, RSI below 80.
  3. Rank: risk-adjusted momentum = (50% 6-month + 30% 3-month + 20% 1-month return) / daily volatility (ATR %). Rewards steady winners over lottery tickets.
  4. Every Monday: hold the top 3 in equal dollar amounts. Anything that falls out of the top 3 is sold and replaced. No buys within 3 days of earnings.
  5. Every day: 8% hard stop per stock (checked 4x a day). Account down 10% = no new buys; down 13% = everything to cash.
  6. Why this rulebook: in the 2-year portfolio simulation it beat every swing variant and QQQ in both halves of history. The cost is big swings: without the account stop its worst drop was about 37%. Details in the Strategy lab.

Rules-based signals for personal use, not financial advice. No strategy guarantees profits; every trade can lose money. Size positions so a stop-out never hurts.
